# Image credits

- `verrill-architeuthis-1882.webp`: A. E. Verrill, *Architeuthis dux*
  (syn. *A. princeps*), 1882, from his work on the giant squids in the
  *Transactions of the Connecticut Academy of Arts and Sciences*. Public domain
  (Wikimedia Commons, "Architeuthis dux Verrill 1882.jpg",
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Architeuthis_dux_Verrill_1882.jpg).
  Resized to 1800 px and re-encoded as WebP. Used as the masthead of
  `about.html` (the newspaper-style About page). Copied from
  `docs/kb/plans/assets/kraken/`, where it was first tried as a watermark under
  the dashboard's signal-flow chart and then taken out.

- The lanternfish icon (`ic-lantern` in `src/Triggerfish/Fish.js` and the
  sprite copied into `about.html`) is a drawing after the plate of
  *Myctophum punctatum* on Wikimedia Commons, "Myctophum punctatum1.jpg",
  public domain (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Myctophum_punctatum1.jpg).
  Traced by eye in the plate's own pixel coordinates (viewBox 88 36 1180 344);
  the plate itself is not shipped.
