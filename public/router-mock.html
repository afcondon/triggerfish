Routing, as matrices

A mock-up from the default routing table. Rows say whose, columns say where (named once), and a cell holds only what varies: a channel, an envelope, a jack. Hover a cell for its crosshair; empty columns are places you could send to.

Notes

melodic and harmonic voices · cell = channel or envelope

⚠ The table says Odonus plays channels 1–4; the rig actually sends 12–15, hard-coded. A matrix that shows the truth needs the rig to route from it.

Drums

hits per lane · cell = jack, voice or sample (• = the lane's own note)

Harmony

what Odonus quantises to · one source per input

Why three. They differ in grain: a voice is a stream on a channel; a drum lane is a single note among sixteen sharing one channel; harmony is a pitch set over time with one source per input. One grid for all three forces every row to repeat what only differs between kinds.

What moved out of the grid. Trims (the flam offset on a doubled kick), sample windows, Rample slots: details of one connection, shown when its cell is opened, not printed on every row.
